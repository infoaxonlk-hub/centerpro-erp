# FSN stock report

Install the three files in this archive over the repository on the main branch. No database migration is needed. Open Stock and choose Fast / Slow / Non-moving. The existing center, location, category, product, quality and search controls filter this report too.

The default window is 90 days, with 30 and 180 day choices. Classification within the selected window: Fast = at least four distinct sales orders and positive net sold quantity; Slow = one to three sales orders and positive net sold quantity; Non-moving = zero net sold quantity. Approved sales returns and sale cancellations reduce net sold quantity. The display is limited to products and locations currently shown in Stock explorer; an entirely depleted item is outside this stock balance report.
