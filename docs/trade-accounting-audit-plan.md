4. [IRD circular index](https://www.ird.gov.lk/en/publications/sitepages/Circulars.aspx?menuid=1506) identifies SEC/2026/E/03 (20 May 2026), revised invoice implementation. Its earlier July date must be read with the August gazette.

Verification limitation: retrieval of the underlying [2481/22 English PDF](https://www.ird.gov.lk/en/publications/Gazette_Documents/2026_2481-22_E.pdf), consolidated VAT Act and 2026 Amendment Act timed out. Consequently exact field placement, numbering/reset rules, time-of-supply/advance rules, credit/debit-note requirements, retention and any API reporting obligations are **not certified by this audit**. Obtain/read those official texts and later IRD notices before finalizing rules, numbering or compliant tax printing. Capture source URL, effective date, review date and rule version in a compliance checklist. Never apply 18% indiscriminately to agricultural products/services; determine each supply's legal classification and recoverability. SSCL is not automatically a customer VAT line.

Proposed document distinction: quotation/pro-forma, commercial non-tax invoice, legally qualified VAT tax invoice, payment receipt, supplier bill, and linked financial credit/debit note. Tax eligibility depends on issuer/recipient registration and supply classification at the legally relevant date, not a user-controlled title switch. Non-tax invoice does not necessarily mean no tax liability (e.g. consumer supply); resolve its calculation/presentation from the Act. Preserve original tax basis on credits rather than selecting today's rate.

## Main separation risks and design decisions

1. **Duplicate posting:** keeping 025/020 confirmation or 024 automatic invoice creation while adding GRN/delivery/billing would post stock or AR/AP twice. Use separate advanced document tables/RPCs; retain old combined entrypoints for simple transactions. Do not insert an advanced commercial SO into legacy `sales_orders` and accidentally fire its invoice trigger.
2. **Implicit source matching:** allocation/valuation/sack triggers dispatch on reference type and sometimes identify a line by product/location/quantity/cost. Introduce explicit immutable inventory document-line identity before advanced posting. Extend dispatch deliberately; unknown reference types must fail closed. Repeated equal-quantity lines must remain distinguishable.
3. **Invoicing after receipt:** inventory arrives before a supplier liability is billed. GRNI/receipt clearing is required, with quantity matching and price/landed-cost variance allocation between remaining inventory and already consumed/sold stock. Never rewrite original movements or completed production snapshots.
4. **Balance copies:** existing order, invoice and receivable/payable balances are updated together. Advanced open items should have one authoritative ledger; legacy fields remain compatibility projections for legacy records, not another independent balance engine.
5. **Returns and reversals:** a physical return need not coincide with a financial credit or refund. Separate all three; preserve original invoice face value and tax. Existing unapplied credits must not disappear or become cash automatically.
6. **Offline/retry:** existing sale/purchase requests are idempotent, but separate inventory, billing and settlement actions need independent request keys, payload hashes and unique source-effect keys. Reject changed payload for reused key. Entire validation, stock, valuation, subledger and optional GL effects commit or roll back together.
7. **Costing/backdating:** preserve the current physical-lot valuation semantics initially. True financial FIFO/pooled AVCO needs a separately accepted design, migration and recosting policy. Disallow backdated inventory postings that would silently recost closed history; use approved current-period adjustments.
8. **Accounting optionality:** advanced purchase and sales work without GL enabled, retaining inventory/valuation and AR/AP subledgers. GL consumes the same immutable posting events only when accounting is enabled and initialized. Enabling accounting requires a reconciled cutoff; never replay history by default. Disabling it after activity requires controlled suspension/export, not silent loss of financial events. Allow entitled users to read/archive outstanding documents and resolve them under a defined suspension policy.
9. **Legacy accounting coverage:** a business-wide accounting claim requires adapters for simple trades, expenses, cash transfers, bank funding, stock adjustments/transfers/scrap, returns/cancellations and manufacturing. Until every active source reconciles, label accounting as pilot/incomplete and block production activation.

## Proposed documents, states and model

Keep legacy table semantics. Add typed `trade_rfqs`/lines, supplier quotations if comparing offers, `trade_purchase_orders`/lines, `trade_quotations`/lines, `trade_sales_orders`/lines, `inventory_documents`/lines, `supplier_bills`/lines and `trade_invoices`/lines. Financial credit/debit notes and payments/collections are separate headers/lines/allocations. Names may be refined before implementation; avoid an unconstrained all-purpose JSON transaction table.

| Document | States and transition meaning |
| --- | --- |
| RFQ / quotation | draft → sent → accepted / rejected / expired / cancelled. Revision creates a version; acceptance links to an order. No stock/GL. |
| PO / SO | draft → submitted → approved/confirmed → closed; cancelled only for unfulfilled remainder. Fulfillment and billing statuses (none/partial/full) derived independently from validated/posted allocations. |
| GRN / delivery / physical return | draft → ready → validated. Cancel before validation; after validation use linked validated reversal/return with quantity ceilings. No editing posted quantities. |
| Supplier bill / invoice / financial note | draft → approved → posted → reversed through linked note/journal. Payment status and remaining credit derived separately. Official number assigned on posting; never reused. |
| Payment / collection | draft → posted → reversed; allocation state unallocated/partial/full. Bank instrument state pending/cleared/dishonoured separately. |
| Journal | draft → posted → reversed by a separately posted journal. Locked periods reject financial changes, including source postings and reversals. |

Shared headers carry organization, center where appropriate, immutable number/series, currency (initially LKR), document/business date, timestamps, creator/approver/validator/poster, source workflow, version and idempotency identity. Posted lines freeze descriptions, UOM, quantities, prices/discounts, tax classification/base/rate/amount, legal party/issuer/address/terms snapshots and rule versions. Snapshot changes require a revision before posting or an adjustment after posting. Drafts may refresh master defaults explicitly; reprints never refresh snapshots.

Add typed allocation tables for PO-line→GRN-line, GRN-line→bill-line, quotation-line→SO-line, SO-line→delivery-line, delivery-line→invoice-line, original-line→return/note-line, and payment→open-item. Support many-to-many consolidation within the same organization/party/currency and permitted centers. Every FK must validate common tenant; allocation sums are locked and checked under concurrency. Distinguish ordered, accepted, rejected, received, returned, delivered, billed and credited quantities. Rejected receipt quantities do not enter usable stock. Backorders are remaining unfulfilled order quantities, with explicit close-short reason; returns do not silently reopen orders.

Extend existing customer/supplier rows additively to preserve IDs and center rules. Add party address rows, effective registration history, payment terms and a common typed party-reference abstraction. Avoid a forced party-table replacement in Phase 1. Organization legal/tax profile needs the same effective history as trading parties.

Accounting foundation: `gl_accounts`, `accounting_periods`, `journal_entries`/`journal_lines`, effective account mappings, `posting_events`/effects, open items and settlement allocations, tax codes/rate versions/registration periods, document series and audit events. Use same-tenant composite relationships where practical. A unique `(organization, source document, source version, effect kind)` prevents duplicate effects; each stock effect also identifies its line and valuation layer. A finalized journal requires at least two valid lines, nonnegative one-sided amounts, exact debit=credit in currency, and same-tenant accounts. Enforce in a controlled transaction with database constraints/deferred checks; posted headers/lines cannot be mutated or deleted.

Each document has its own list/detail/print view. Related-document panels show source/target links, allocated quantities/values, remaining quantities, posting events, journals, payments, credits and reversal chains. Links respect permissions; hidden GL access must not leak balances through counts or joined data.

## Proposed posting matrix

The following is the target accrual policy, subject to tax point and revenue-recognition verification. Inventory valuation always posts with a validated physical document; GL effects apply only to initialized accounting businesses.

| Validated/posted event | Stock / valuation | Debit | Credit | AR/AP/open-item effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RFQ, quotation, PO, SO | None | None | None | None |
| GRN, accepted quantity | Increase at provisional receipt value; lot/sack records | Inventory | Goods received not invoiced (GRNI) | None |
| Matched supplier bill | No quantity; separately valued difference adjustment if necessary | GRNI matched value; recoverable input tax; inventory/expense variance as applicable | Supplier AP gross | Full gross AP, then separately allocate payments/credits |
| Supplier payment | None | AP, or supplier advance if unallocated | Cash / bank / instrument payable clearing | Allocation reduces AP once |
| Validated delivery | Decrease at actual allocated carrying value | COGS (or deferred cost if recognition policy requires it) | Inventory | None |
| Invoice | No stock | Customer AR gross | Revenue net; output tax | Full gross AR |
| Collection | None | Cash / bank / card/cheque clearing | AR, or customer advance if unallocated | Allocation reduces AR once |
| Customer physical return | Increase from original delivery allocation at original cost | Inventory | COGS / deferred cost | None |
| Customer credit note | None | Sales returns/revenue reduction; eligible output-tax reversal | AR / customer credit liability | Reduce due or create unapplied credit; no automatic refund |
| Supplier physical return | Decrease at current carrying value, record difference from commercial credit value | Supplier return clearing; variance as needed | Inventory; variance as needed | None |
| Supplier credit note | No quantity | AP / supplier credit asset | Supplier return clearing; eligible input-tax reversal; price adjustment as applicable | Reduce payable or retain supplier credit |
| Refund / credit payout | None | Customer credit liability (customer refund) | Cash/bank | Consume credit once; supplier refund is Dr cash/bank, Cr supplier credit asset |
| GRN/delivery reversal | Exact inverse linked allocation, subject to availability and downstream dependencies | Reverse original account effects | Reverse original account effects | No unrelated financial reversal |
| Bill/invoice correction | No physical movement | Linked financial reversal/adjustment | Linked financial reversal/adjustment | Reverse/adjust open item; preserve original face document |
| Manufacturing completion | Inputs out, output lots in at frozen actual cost | WIP for materials/conversion; finished inventory on completion | Raw/packaging inventory, conversion accrual or expense reclassification; WIP on completion | Conversion accrual is explicit; do not pay recorded expense twice |
| Stock adjustment/scrap/transfer | Validated quantities at actual value; transfer value conserved | Inventory or loss/adjustment accounts, by direction | Inventory or gain/adjustment accounts, by direction | None; internal transfer has no revenue/profit |
| Card/cheque clearance | None | Bank or clearing, by direction | Clearing or bank, by direction | Settlement/dishonour policy; no second collection/payment |

Taxes not recoverable belong in inventory/expense cost, not input-tax receivable. Advances may create tax at a legally determined point; do not defer all VAT to final invoice without verifying the Act. Receipt/bill price variance must be attributable to receipt allocations; if stock was consumed into production or sold, post traceable adjustment effects instead of modifying locked source costs. Two-decimal journal rounding versus six-decimal valuation needs an explicit rounding account and exact reconciliation bridge.

## Migration and reconciliation strategy

1. **Preflight, read-only:** compare deployed migration catalog and schema to ordered local replay when separately authorized. Inventory all triggers/function overloads/grants, source types, missing/duplicate allocations, negative stock, null values, cancelled/returned docs, unallocated credits, open cheques/cards and held/offline drafts. Export per-business/center/location/product/quality/lot quantity and value and per-party open balances as of a defined cutoff.
2. **Additive foundation:** new nullable master columns/tables and disabled-by-default entitlement keys. Do not backfill TIN/VAT status, historical addresses or terms by guessing. Preserve names, anonymous purchases and walk-in customers. Validate master corrections separately and retain audit history.
3. **Keep history labeled legacy:** do not manufacture historical GRNs, deliveries or statutory tax invoices that trigger posting. If traceability needs synthetic links, create nonposting `legacy_import` references with provenance and known/unknown snapshot fields. Do not relabel old receipt numbers as legally compliant tax invoice numbers.
4. **Stock cutoff:** authoritative opening is signed movement quantities and exact 056 inventory values, reconciled to signed lot allocations and sacks. Existing Legacy/unassigned stock remains explicitly unassigned. No new opening stock movement when reusing the existing inventory ledger. Only approved differences create a new adjustment; quarantine unresolved discrepancies.
5. **AR/AP cutoff:** reconcile sales order credit, invoice due and nonvoid receivable, and purchase credit versus nonvoid payable; trace settlements and applied/unapplied returns. Separate cancelled documents. Import only outstanding open items and unapplied credits with source identity, not gross invoice history plus outstanding again. Preserve customer center restrictions and supplier organization scope. Report differences before any opening posting.
6. **Accounting opening:** one immutable, balanced opening batch per business/cutoff/version: inventory, AR, AP, verified cash/bank, advances/credits, card/cheque clearing, GRNI if genuinely outstanding, accruals, tax and equity/opening adjustment. Reconcile control accounts to open items and valuation. Existing cash clearing/variance balances require investigation; do not copy both legacy cash ledgers and their 039 derived entries. Obtain business sign-off on unexplained opening differences before activation.
7. **Event boundary:** pre-cutoff economics appear only in the opening GL; later events post once from adapters. A later collection of a legacy invoice settles its imported open item without reposting revenue; a later legacy return uses original stock allocations and explicit GL cutoff treatment. Drain or version old offline queues before enforcing a new cutoff; reject unsupported stale payloads with recoverable drafts.
8. **Rehearsal:** replay full history and new migrations on isolated PostgreSQL/Supabase fixtures, rerun backfill to prove idempotency, compare quantity/value/balance checksums and tenant isolation. Never run migration tests against remote production credentials. Use short validated migrations and plan lock/backfill duration.
9. **Rollout later:** enable per business only after reconciliation and user acceptance. Disable new creation on rollback, preserve posted documents/events and use reversals; never drop financial history. Preview/remote migration/deployment remain out of scope for this task and need a later authorized stage.

## Small implementation phases and acceptance tests

### Phase 1: exact scope — party masters, tax configuration, accounting foundation

Deliver as small dependent changes, with no new purchase/sales workflow or changes to existing stock/payment posting:

1. **Party and issuer foundation:** additive customer/supplier/company legal fields (registered/trading name, registration identifier, TIN, email/phones), structured billing/delivery/registered addresses, payment terms and effective VAT registration history. Preserve customer-center and supplier-business scope. Add controlled master create/edit RPCs and audit history. Do not make tax identifiers mandatory for anonymous legacy cash trades. No automatic merging/deduplication.
2. **Tax configuration foundation:** effective tax codes/rates, supply classification, recoverability and inclusive/exclusive policy fields; no overlapping active rule periods. Registration status remains separate from TIN. Store official source/review metadata. Add document-type eligibility and snapshot-building/validation services for future posted documents. Final legal rules/series activation depend on completing the official-text checklist above. No active tax calculation added to legacy POS and no claim of compliant tax printing in this phase.
3. **Accounting schema and engine:** business chart of accounts with inventory/GRNI/AR/AP/cash/bank/tax/revenue/COGS/variance/clearing/advances/WIP mappings, periods and close/reopen audit, balanced immutable journal posting and reversal primitives, event uniqueness and opening-batch design. No automatic legacy or advanced transaction journaling, no opening-balance imports and no accounting activation before adapter/reconciliation phases. Internal posting helpers inaccessible to clients; preparer/poster/period-manager permissions separate.
4. **Access and language plumbing:** independently disabled `advanced_purchases`, `advanced_sales`, `accounting` entitlements in database and platform catalog/API; explicit RPC checks, table RLS and cross-tenant FK guards. Only Super Admin can change entitlements, not business admins. Add translation keys/message catalogs with English fallback and future `si`/`ta` support for every new label, error/status presentation and print text. Database errors use stable codes and parameters mapped by UI; persist canonical statuses, not translated strings.
5. **Read-only setup UI:** party/issuer editing, tax version configuration, chart/mapping/period setup behind permissions. No advanced navigation to usable trade workflows yet. Accounting setup clearly indicates not initialized; public manual journal entry and live period enforcement on unintegrated legacy postings are deferred.

Phase 1 acceptance:

- Existing party IDs, orders, balances and stock are unchanged; simple purchases/sales, walk-in/cash-farmer, sacks, lots, labor and offline retries retain behavior.
