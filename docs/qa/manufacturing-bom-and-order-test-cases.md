# Manufacturing BoM and order QA

Apply 057 after 056 in a test database. Use an active Manufacturing/Inventory business, authorized admin, center-scoped staff, two active centers with multiple locations, an inactive location and another tenant. Seed raw material, rice and packaging stock through purchases/transfers/adjustments. [Setup and workflow](../manufacturing-bom-workflow.md).

| Scenario | Steps | Expected result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Create milling BoM | Configure baseline 6 KG rice from 10 KG paddy; 2 KG bran; main/bran shares 75%/25%; milling 1 hour × 5 and handling per actual KG × 1. Choose source, finished and bran destinations. | Saves name/code, units, component/operation/by-product plans and destinations. No source lot is stored. No stock/cost posting occurs. |
| Edit/convert | Edit plans/rates/destinations and save. Open a legacy operation preset and fill its finished product, source and components. | Revision increments; new orders use the new plan. Existing draft snapshots and completed orders remain unchanged. Legacy presets remain identifiable until converted. |
| Matching product | Select a finished product on New milling and inspect matching BoMs. Try wrong product/process and inactive BoM. | Only matching active BoMs are offered. Server validates finished product identity and permissions independently. |
| Scaling | Select production target 12 KG and load the 6-KG baseline. | Planned paddy 20 KG, main 12 KG, bran 4 KG, milling 2 hours and handling 20 KG; units and destinations remain correct. |
| Actual consumption | Enter actual paddy 22 KG from a 10/KG lot, actual milling 3 hours and accepted bran 3.5 KG. | Plan stays visible; actuals/deviations survive save/reopen. Production cost is 220 material + 15 hours + 22 handling = 257, using actual quantities and BoM rules. |
| Actual lots | Select lots on component rows, save, reopen and complete. | Actual lot IDs/numbers remain traceable through input movement → order → finished/by-product lot. BoM never acquires a source lot. |
| Wrong/insufficient/inactive source | Send another product/quality/tenant's lot, a lot stocked only in another source center/location, or too much quantity. Deactivate product, quality or location after saving a draft. | Invalid identity is rejected on save; availability/activity is rechecked on completion. Failure leaves stock, sacks and costing unchanged. The registry has no independent inactive-lot switch. |
| Explicit Legacy | Choose Legacy for tracked stock. Then request more than the Legacy pool while other lots have enough total stock. | Only Legacy stock is consumed. It cannot fall through to another lot. Tracked positive consumption cannot complete with automatic/unselected source. Drafts may await selection. |
| Actual labor/rate protection | Change actual hours/batch quantity; send an altered rate or invoke legacy save on a structured order. | Actual cost follows immutable BoM rules. Per-KG actual is derived from actual material KG. Rate overrides and legacy replacement are rejected; multiple operations are charged once. |
| Snapshot/reload | Save revision-1 draft, edit reusable BoM to revision 2, reopen draft, change target without reloading, and then explicitly reload. | Revision 1 plan/rates/actuals are retained until confirmed reload. Changed selections cannot save without loading the replacement. Rerenders do not overwrite actuals. |
| Destinations | Configure finished goods to a shelf and bran to another accessible center. Complete. | Each positive accepted output posts to the exact configured location, with correct lot/value and source traceability. |
| Invalid destination | Choose a location belonging to another center/business, deactivate it after draft save, or override destination in order JSON. | Save/complete rejects it; no partial stock/value posting. Destinations are fixed by the snapshot. |
| Zero by-product | With zero/proportional shares, enter by-product actual zero. Repeat with positive cost share and zero accepted quantity. | Zero actual remains auditable without a zero-quantity receipt. Positive allocated share with zero quantity fails. |
| Packing BoM | Configure 2 × 5-KG packs from 10 KG rice plus 2 bags and 1 packing hour. Stage rice and bags in the source location. | Loads rice/packaging plans without fixed lots; requires one rice variety and a valid finished-goods destination. |
| Actual packing | Enter 10.5 KG actual rice, 3 actual bags, 2 actual hours and 2 accepted packs. Complete. | Actual rice and packaging are consumed once; output receives their actual costs and labor. Rice process loss is 0.5 KG and is displayed/audited. |
| Packing variety/weight | Try another rice variety or rice consumption below accepted packed weight. | Rejected. Actual overconsumption is explicit process loss for BoM orders; existing manual packing rules remain unchanged. |
| Draft/retry/rollback | Compare movements/costs/payments/COGS before and after draft changes, completion and lost-response retries. Force shortage or invalid linked expense. | Drafts post/reserve nothing. Completion is atomic and retries reuse the stable order ID. Failures preserve pre-completion stock and the draft. |
| Cost/P&L compatibility | Inspect the 257-cost order’s output allocations; sell part of a produced lot. Link a recorded operation expense on another order. | 056’s exact costs are capitalized; unsold output stays inventory, sales COGS follows its lot value, and recorded expenses are not counted twice. |
| Security | Use source-only staff, another tenant, expired/disabled Manufacturing, direct child-table writes and internal legacy RPCs. | Inaccessible multi-center BoMs are not exposed as partial plans. Tenant/center/role/module/subscription checks hold. Raw writes and internal helper calls are denied. |
| Existing behavior | Exercise manual legacy manufacturing, purchases with multiple weighings/one product lot, sales/offline retry, stock transfers, sacks and P&L. | Existing supported workflows still work without duplicate tables, receipts, expenses or valuations. |

Automated tests use isolated PostgreSQL/hook fixtures. Real browser and authenticated Preview QA is separate and must be run after migration.
